About me

This page is personification of my mind and soul. Everything I think, feel, and perceive, I write. Writing, for me, is like stacking the strewed blocks into a castle, gates, towers, and what not.

As much as I love writing, I love reading even more. I read to fill myself up and write to empty myself out. This cycle has taught me that literature doesn't belong to the one who writes but to the one who reads. The open interpertaion of pieces that I write are what keeps them alive and I am trying to keep them alive.
