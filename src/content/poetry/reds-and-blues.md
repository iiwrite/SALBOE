I went to lands unknown  
To collect rubies on my own  
For each ruby found, I carved  
Into a heart, with a piece of me inside.

Adorned with roses in a box,  
And ribbons wrapped all around.  
I gave them all to you  
One by one, into your waiting palm.

Love glimmered in your eyes,  
With a subtle shade of qualm.

In the search for rubies,  
I found sapphires.  
With all the sapphires found,  
I forged them into armor.

I wore the armor over my heart.  
It kept me safe when you smiled.  
When my heart skipped with your kiss,  
It did not let its guard down, even for a while.

Yet it failed when you chose to leave.  
I am left with sapphire's melt as I grieve.  
All the red gifted, only the blue remains with me.  
Take the blue too, leave me colourless, set me free.
