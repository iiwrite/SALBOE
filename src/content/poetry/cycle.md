I sit and stare at a blank-lit screen  
The vertical line blinks at a metronome's beat  
Almost like rhythm of my heart.

I wait to write that perfect line  
A line, that makes buried wound bleed  
A line, that awakens rivers long run dry  
A line, that will survive the test of time.

Ideas swirl like autumn leaves in a gust  
I hunt them down and snatch one  
Glue it onto the canvas  
Only to peel it off.

Ideas swirl again like autumn leaves in a gust  
I glue one onto the canvas, again, and again  
Only to erase it clean again.

Time slips by, day turns into night  
The vertical line still blinks at a metronome's beat  
I turn away from the screen.  
Until it slowly glows, dims, and dissolves in darkness.
